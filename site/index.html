A koi pond for your website background.

Dithered koi swim quietly behind your content. Point at one and it darts away.

Add it to your site →

Drop it in

Pick whichever fits your site. Your page stays clickable: the pond sits behind everything and only reads where the pointer is.

Add this before </body>. That's it.

<script src="koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond></script>

Options go in data- attributes:

<script src="koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond
        data-mode="lines" data-count="14" data-pixel-size="2"></script>

Real koi colours on dark pond water, or on white:

<script src="koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond data-colors="koi"></script>
<script src="koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond data-colors="koi" data-background="#ffffff"></script>

Import it from dist/koi-pond.esm.js, or from your bundler once installed.

import { createKoiPond } from './koi-pond.esm.js';

const pond = createKoiPond({
  ink: '#1a42e2',
  background: '#eff5fd',
  mode: 'bayer',
});

// later
pond.set({ mode: 'lines', count: 14 });
pond.destroy();

Give it a target and the pond fills just that element (for example a hero) instead of the whole page.

<section id="hero">…</section>

<script src="koi-pond.min.js"></script>
<script>
  KoiPond.create({ target: '#hero', count: 6 });
</script>

Using it as a full-page background? The canvas sits at z-index: -1. If your body has its own background colour, move that colour to html (or remove it) so the pond can show through.

How it works

  1. 01

    Swim

    Each koi is a chain of points, so turns bend the whole body. Like real carp they swim in bursts: a few tail beats, then a long glide. A wave runs from head to tail and on into the fin, and the side fins tuck in at speed and spread out to brake and hover.

  2. 02

    Shade

    Each fish is painted as a real koi in a tiny offscreen image, one pixel per dot: markings on the back (17 real varieties: kohaku, sanke, showa, utsuri, bekko, goromo, goshiki, asagi, shusui, kumonryu, kujaku, ogon and more), a body lit along the spine and darker down the flanks, a net of scales, eyes and see-through fins.

  3. 03

    Dither

    In the blue look, each fish's brightness picks the dot density: black markings become deep blue, red mid blue, white skin pale. Between two of five tones, an 8×8 Bayer matrix decides which tone each dot gets.

The only interaction: when your pointer is on a fish, it startles like a real fish. A sharp bend to one side, a burst of strong tail beats, then a long glide back to calm.

Meet the koi

Every variety in the pond, drawn live by the same shader. Switch between the two styles.

    Options

    OptionDefaultWhat it does
    targetdocument.bodyElement or selector to fill. The body gives a fixed full-page background.
    canvasnullUse your own <canvas> instead (sized by your CSS).
    count10Number of koi.
    fishSize'auto'Body length in CSS pixels. Auto scales with the viewport.
    fishScale1Size multiplier for the fish (0.5 = half, 2 = double). Changing it with pond.set() resizes the swimming fish smoothly.
    butterfly0Share of butterfly koi with long, flowing fins: 0.2 is about 1 in 5, 1 is all of them.
    varieties'all'Which koi swim: 'all', or names like ['kohaku', 'showa', 'ogon']. Names: kohaku, showa, asagi, ogon, tancho, shiro-utsuri, hi-utsuri, ki-utsuri, shiro-bekko, ai-goromo, goshiki, shusui, kumonryu, ochiba, kujaku, hariwake, soragoi.
    colors'ink''ink' for one ink colour, or 'koi' for real koi colours: red, black and white Kohaku, Sanke and Showa, Tancho, blue-grey Asagi, metallic gold and platinum Ogon, brown Chagoi.
    ink'#1a42e2'Dot colour when colors is 'ink' (hex or rgb()).
    background'auto'Light paper (#eff5fd) for ink, dark pond water (#0d2a30) for real koi colours. Or any colour, or 'transparent'. Both colour modes work on light and dark backgrounds.
    mode'bayer''bayer' dots or 'lines' line screen.
    tones4Shades of ink between paper and full ink. 1 gives single-colour dots.
    pixelSize2Size of one dot in CSS pixels.
    speed1Swimming speed multiplier.
    dartBoost1.5Extra speed when a fish darts (1.5 means up to 2.5× normal).
    dartDuration1Seconds until a dart has mostly faded.
    interactivetrueTurn the pointer reaction off.
    reducedMotion'auto'Slows the pond down for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
    maxDpr2Upper limit for device pixel ratio.
    zIndex-1z-index of the full-page canvas.
    seednullA number gives the same pond every time.

    Methods

    • pond.set({ … }) change any option while it runs
    • pond.addFish({ variety: 'kohaku', longFin: true }) add one koi that swims in from the edge
    • pond.pause() / pond.resume()
    • pond.destroy() stop and remove the canvas
