Koi that swim behind your website.

A small dithering shader that turns any page into a koi pond. Point at a fish and it darts away, or click the water.

Add it to your site

錦鯉

Drop it in

One script tag adds the pond behind any page. Your page stays clickable: the canvas only reads where the pointer is.

WebGL2, about 15 KB gzipped, no dependencies, MIT licensed.

Add this before </body>.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Hiruna01/ascii-koi@main/dist/koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond></script>

Options go in data- attributes, for example real koi colours on white:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Hiruna01/ascii-koi@main/dist/koi-pond.min.js" data-koi-pond
        data-colors="koi" data-background="#ffffff" data-count="14"></script>

Import it from dist/koi-pond.esm.js.

import { createKoiPond } from './koi-pond.esm.js';

const pond = createKoiPond({ colors: 'koi', count: 12 });

// later
pond.set({ mode: 'lines', fishScale: 1.4 });
pond.destroy();

Give it a target and the pond fills only that element.

<section id="hero">...</section>

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Hiruna01/ascii-koi@main/dist/koi-pond.min.js"></script>
<script>
  KoiPond.create({ target: '#hero', count: 6 });
</script>

As a full-page background the canvas sits at z-index: -1. If your body has a background colour, move it to html so the pond shows through.

How it works

  1. Swim

    Each koi is a chain of points, so a turn bends the whole body. They swim like real carp: a few tail beats, then a long glide. Point at one and it startles, then calms down.

  2. Paint

    Every fish is painted as a real koi into a small offscreen image: markings on the back, a body lit along the spine, a net of scales, eyes and see-through fins.

  3. Dither

    The brightness of that painting picks one of five tones for each dot, and an 8×8 Bayer matrix decides between the two nearest. Black becomes deep blue, red mid blue, white a pale fill.

Meet the koi

Every variety in the pond, drawn live by the same shader.

    Options

    Pass any of these to KoiPond.create(), or change them later with pond.set().

    Look

    colors'ink'
    'ink' for one ink colour, 'koi' for real koi colours.
    ink'#1a42e2'
    Dot colour in the ink look.
    background'auto'
    Paper for ink, dark pond for koi colours, or any colour, or 'transparent'.
    mode'bayer'
    'bayer' dots or a 'lines' screen.
    tones4
    Shades of ink between paper and full ink.
    pixelSize2
    Size of one dot in CSS pixels.
    waterfalse
    Faint light on the water, and ripples where you click.
    waterAmount1
    How visible the water is.

    Fish

    count10
    Number of koi.
    varieties'all'
    Or names, like ['kohaku', 'showa', 'ogon'].
    fishScale1
    Size multiplier. Changing it resizes the fish as they swim.
    fishSize'auto'
    Body length in CSS pixels.
    butterfly0
    Share of koi with long, flowing fins.

    Motion

    speed1
    Swimming speed multiplier.
    interactivetrue
    Startle a fish when the pointer is on it.
    ripplestrue
    With water on, a click on an empty spot sends out ripples. Nearby fish flee them.
    dartBoost1.5
    Extra speed when startled (up to 2.5×).
    dartDuration1
    Seconds until a startle has faded.
    reducedMotion'auto'
    Slows the pond for visitors who prefer less motion.

    Placement

    targetbody
    Element or selector to fill. The body gives a full-page background.
    canvasnull
    Use your own <canvas>.
    zIndex-1
    Stacking order of the full-page canvas.
    maxDpr2
    Upper limit for device pixel ratio.
    seednull
    A number gives the same pond every time.

    Methods

    • pond.set({ ... }) changes options while it runs.
    • pond.addFish({ variety: 'kohaku' }) adds one koi that swims in from the edge.
    • pond.ripple(x, y) sends out a ripple at a point.
    • pond.pause() and pond.resume()
    • pond.destroy() removes the canvas.
